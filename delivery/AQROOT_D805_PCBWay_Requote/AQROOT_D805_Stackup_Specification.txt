AQROOT D-805 - PCB STACKUP SPECIFICATION
Board: aqroot-Beta-v2
Layers: 6
Nominal finished thickness: 1.6 mm
Surface finish: ENIG
Laminate: FR-4, Tg >= 150 C
Impedance control: not required
Minimum finished drill: 0.20 mm

Preferred layer build:
L1  Copper    35 um (1 oz)
    Prepreg   0.2104 mm, 7628
L2  Copper    15.2 um (0.5 oz)
    Core      0.4000 mm, FR-4
L3  Copper    15.2 um (0.5 oz)
    Prepreg   0.2028 mm, 7628
L4  Copper    15.2 um (0.5 oz)
    Core      0.4000 mm, FR-4
L5  Copper    15.2 um (0.5 oz)
    Prepreg   0.2104 mm, 7628
L6  Copper    35 um (1 oz)

Target calculated stack thickness: 1.5744 mm
Acceptable finished-board thickness: 1.5744 +/- 0.10 mm.

Process requirements:
- Via-in-pad / land-intersecting vias must be resin filled, planarized and copper capped (POFV/VIPPO or equivalent planar solderable process).
- Preserve plated slots, NPTH features, board outline and Gerber geometry.
- Do not alter copper geometry without written approval.
- If this exact dielectric build is not available, please propose the closest manufacturable 6-layer equivalent while preserving 1 oz outer copper, 0.5 oz inner copper, nominal 1.6 mm finished thickness, Tg >= 150 C and ENIG. Please identify any deviation before fabrication.
