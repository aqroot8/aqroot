3. Apparent trace crossings in your CAM screenshots — NONE IS A SHORT
----------------------------------------------------------------------
We checked the D-804 Gerbers directly (using the X2 net attributes in the files): zero
different-net crossings, zero different-net overlaps, zero shorts, on every copper layer.

(a) Layer overlay. Signals are routed on L1, L3, L4 and L6. When several copper layers are
    shown together, traces on different layers appear to cross; they only connect at
    drilled vias. Please view one copper layer at a time.
(b) Same-net crossings. There are 133 places where two trace segments of the SAME net cross
    or overlap on the same layer (L1: 32, L3: 15, L4: 1, L6: 85), including some exactly
    duplicated segments. They are one electrical node, so they are not shorts. Please build
    them as supplied; do not remove or merge them. Each location, with net name and
    coordinates: AQROOT_D804_same_net_crossings.csv.

4. Open-ended traces in your CAM screenshots
----------------------------------------------------------------------
(a) Three trace ends in the earlier files did not end on a pad or another trace end. The
    copper overlapped, so they were electrically connected, but a CAM end-point check
    correctly flags them. All three are corrected in D-804 by adding one short joining
    segment of the same net and width (nothing removed):
      L3 (In2), net EXT_SDA_BUF: two trace ends at Gerber X 60.150 and X 60.300,
        Y -40.800 (0.15 mm apart) — now joined.
      L6 (Bottom), net BQ25185_SYS: trace end at Gerber X 57.150, Y -37.900, 0.075 mm
        outside pad L4.1 — now extended onto the pad.
    The D-804 Gerbers have no trace end that does not land on a pad, via, trace or pour,
    on any layer.
(b) Isolated pads (not traces). 46 pads intentionally have no routed connection. Each is
    listed, with its reason, in AQROOT_D804_isolated_pads.csv:
      - 18 pads of Do-Not-Populate parts (U13, L2, R44, R45, C34, C35, R107, R68, R112,
        C81, C82, R123): unrouted by design and NOT assembled (see the BOM / DNP list);
      - TP9 (test pad on the unpopulated U13 rail) and U11 pin 3 (deliberately unconnected
        by design);
      - 26 no-connect pins and mounting pads (J1, J2, J7, J8, U7, U8, U9) with no net.
    No trace stub is attached to any of them.
Please run your netlist compare against the D-804 Gerbers. We expect exactly the pads in
AQROOT_D804_isolated_pads.csv to be unconnected; please report anything else to us.
