## 2. The §5 local screen — validated, and 15× cheaper

§5 asked for a qualification "much cheaper than full Phase A" that still uses
real prefix copper, because PR-40 rules out geometry-only proxies.

`AQROOT_LOCAL=R80` lays **only the west-margin prefix**: the 1.50 mm
`BAT_PROTECTED_P` trunk, the BAT_MAIN chain, the PR-43 `BAT_RAW` bridges, and
U18's eight-pin field — 19 named connections.

That is sufficient because of *how* D-255 actually happens: the copper that
boxes `U18.7` is `LTC_SHDN`'s `U18.6 → R80.2` segment, and **that segment is
inside the U18 pin field itself**, laid between two adjacent pins of the same
package. It does not need the cross-board trip runs to appear.

Everything skipped — the FET sense pairs, the inter-FET gate link, the taps,
the trip network, the dead-cell block, gauge, caps, closure and test points — is
east or south of the contested margin and cannot free a lane in it.

**The instrument was validated before it was trusted.** The control (002F
`R80`/`R81`, PR-43 ON) reproduces D-255 exactly:

    CTRL   U18 6/8   open U18.7, U18.10   R80.1 connected   D12.1 connected
    471 s, against roughly two hours for a full Phase A

Same casualties, same connectivity, **15× faster**. The screen is *conservative*,
never generous: it omits the closure stage, so a pin it reports open may still
close in a full run, but a pin it reports **connected is genuinely connected**.

## 5. The ruling this produces

**Moving `R80`/`R81` is not the lever.** It was a reasonable first hypothesis —
those parts sit in U18's north lanes and their pose had never been searched —
but seven measured placements say the constraint is not where they are.

The §10 reserve does **not** apply. It authorises one via on `LTC4368_FAULT_N`
only if candidates fail *solely* because `U18.7` stays boxed. `U18.7` turns out
to be the **easy** one: five of six candidates close it. What fails is a moving
set — `U18.10`, `U18.6`, `U18.3`, `Q3_CS`, `LTC_GATE` — and a via on FAULT_N
addresses none of it.

**§10's stop condition is met instead:** `LTC_GATE` degrades under every
`R80`/`R81` move (2 islands at K6, 5 at K1, against 1 island at the 002I
baseline). §10 says stop for CTO review, and forbids a via on `LTC_GATE`.

### What the accumulated evidence now says

Across 002I and 002J, **every single intervention has landed at or below 24 of
29**: reordering (PR-43), and now seven placements. The margin is not short of a
lane here or there — it is short of layers. The realistic levers left are:

1. **A second routing layer for the low-current signals.** `LTC_GATE`,
   `LTC4368_FAULT_N`, `LTC_SHDN` and `LTC_OV` are all status/control nets already
   carrying vias. Giving that class a planned F.Cu path — rather than one
   grudging via at a time — is the only lever that adds capacity instead of
   redistributing it.
2. **Relax the zero-via preference on `Q3_CS`** (§12 says "preferred", not
   required), which K6 shows is now a genuine contender for the margin.
3. **Widen the west margin geometrically** — the parts north of U18 are what
   makes it narrow, and that is a bigger ECO than this task authorises.

**Recommendation: (1).** It is the only option that changes the capacity rather
than choosing who loses. It needs a CTO ruling because it is a routing-policy
change, not a placement change.

## 6. PR-44 — a segfault that was killing full Phase A runs

Two full Phase A runs died with SIGSEGV at **exactly** connection 28, in
`apply_areas` → `corridor_from_tracks` → `GetClass()`.

`grow()` stored the `PCB_TRACK` **objects** in each rule area. When a later
connection fails and `qb.revert()` removes its copper, KiCad frees those
objects — and the next `apply_areas()` read freed memory. Deterministic, and it
only bites after the first revert that follows an area growing, which is why it
had never surfaced before this placement.

**Fix: store the UUID and resolve it against the board each time.** The object
is alive when `grow` reads it, and the board is the authority on what still
exists. `area_stats` needed the same treatment.

`router_regression`: **ALL CHECKS PASS** (34 checks, G8-A…G8-F included).

This is a general harness fix and it is the most durable thing this task
produced — without it, no full Phase A on a placement with early reverts can
complete at all.
