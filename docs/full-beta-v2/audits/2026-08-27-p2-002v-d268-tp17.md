# FBV2-P2-002V — D-268: the western-margin partition is routed copper, not placement

**Date:** 2026-08-27 · **Task:** FBV2-P2-002V · **Starting HEAD:** `6ebb009`
**Verdict:** **DECISION STOP** (§19 and §24). TP17-only is measured insufficient,
C58 is measured irrelevant, and every outer-layer zero-via approach fails. The
authoritative PCB is unchanged. No placement ECO, no signal copper written.

---

## 1. D-268 as recorded

> **The high-current BPP trunk remains on outer 1 oz copper with zero vias. The
> first placement lever for the severed western B.Cu plane is TP17, a
> non-functional test point routed last. C58 may move only if TP17-alone is
> measured insufficient. Inner-layer/high-current-via bypass is not authorized.**

The ruling was executed in that order, and the order is what produced the
finding: **the lever was aimed at the wrong thing, and it took one cheap
measurement to prove it.**

## 2. The direct test that came first

Before any placement search, the parts named in 002U's blocker list were removed
from the finished 002U board and connectivity re-tested at every width:

| board | `R75.2 → D9.1` on B.Cu at 1.50 / 1.20 / 0.60 / 0.20 mm |
|---|---|
| as routed (002U) | **no / no / no / no** |
| **TP17 moved away** | **no / no / no / no** |
| **C58 moved away** | **no / no / no / no** |
| TP17 + C58 moved away | no / no / no / no |
| TP17 + C58 + TP15 away | no / no / no / no |

**Neither test point nor the capacitor is on the cut.** Removing them entirely —
not merely relocating them — changes nothing at any width down to 0.20 mm.

instead of 19.878 mm because the control copper is still an obstacle elsewhere.

## 4. Sections 4–7 — TP17 searched and replayed anyway

TP17 is a single 1.00 × 1.00 mm B.Cu pad at (12.500, 76.000) on `LTC_GATE`, with
a 3.92 × 3.32 mm courtyard. 20 legal poses were found outside the R75/D9 current
corridor (x 6.5–15.0, y 62.0–79.0) with no courtyard clash; the ten smallest
displacements were ranked and the best taken.

**Selected: TP17 → (17.000, 79.000), displacement 5.408 mm**, same layer, same
net, same footprint, same serviceable board region, probe face unchanged (B.Cu,
1.00 mm pad, no neighbour within its courtyard).

Full D-266/D-267 prefix replayed on that placement:

- **U18 8/8, all nine PR-40 targets true** — the functional prefix reproduces
- `LTC_SHDN`, `LTC_OV`, `LTC_UV`, `LTC4368_FAULT_N`, `Q3_CS`, `Q2_CS`, `BAT_MID`
  each one component; `LTC_GATE` all six functional pads
- BAT_SENSE current path `{Q3.5, Q3.6, R75.1, U18.9}` one island
- **Kelvin A 7.644 mm / 2 vias, B 9.927 mm / 2 vias, mismatch 2.283 mm**, both In2
- **and `R75.2 → D9.1` = `NO LEGAL ESCAPE at >= 1.200 mm`.**

The blocker list is the proof: 002U read `track (x37), R77.2 (x8), TP17.1 (x5)`;
with TP17 moved it reads **`track (x42), R77.2 (x8), R78…`**. **TP17 left the
list and the trunk did not move.** §7 FAILS.

TP17's move was not entirely without effect — it opened a **0.60 mm** path where
002U had none at any width (53.916 mm). That is real and it is **below the
1.20 mm floor**, so it is not a trunk.

| distance to `C25.1` / `C36.1` (bulk) | 50.122 / 51.021 mm |

A 1 µF X7R 4 mm from the protected-node diode, with the bulk capacitors 50 mm

bulk storage. Its placement is the reason it works.

**C58 was NOT moved, and the reason is measured rather than argued:** removing it
from the board entirely changes `R75.2 → D9.1` at **no width from 1.50 down to
0.20 mm**. §9's bounded search was not spent, because relocating a decoupling
capacitor for zero measured benefit is exactly the electrical penalty §9 says to
stop for.

## 6. Sections 11–13 — BAT_RAW

**`R79.1 → R80.1` ROUTES** — 4.532 mm, 0.20 mm, **F.Cu, 2 vias at the D-267 legal
0.65/0.40**, no clearance or hole rule relaxed. `D12.1 → R77.1` routes 14.637 mm
at 0.60 mm on B.Cu with zero vias; `U18.1 → R77.1` 4.910 mm at 0.20 mm.

**`R77.1 → R79.1` still fails**, rejected on `Clearance violation (rule 'BAT_MAIN
routed clearance')` — 0.300 mm required. **`BAT_RAW` is therefore two functional
groups:** `{C59.1, F1.2, Q2.7, Q2.8, R79.1, R80.1, R86.2, R89.1}` and
`{D12.1, R77.1, U18.1}`. §13 FAILS.

**And this is a familiar shape.** `R77.1` and `R79.1` are D-249 microamp divider
taps carrying nanoamps, held to the **high-current routed clearance** because
they share a net name with the pack current. D-249 fixed exactly this for
**width**; D-264 fixed it for **layer**; D-267 fixed it for **via geometry**.
Clearance is the fourth property and the only one still scoped by net name.
§12 forbids relaxing it here, so it is **raised, not taken**.

## 7. Sections 18 and 24 — the long outer trunk

**On the V1 board the long outer route does not exist either.** Measured
directly: `D9.1 → R75.2` on B.Cu returns **`NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE` at 1.50 mm and at
1.20 mm** — `D9.1`'s own pad exit is gone, behind 42 track segments, `R77.2` and
`R78`. The 45.467 mm route seen in 002U existed **only because the D-267
reservation had preserved that exit**, and §15 forbids leaving such a stub
dangling.

The comparison is recorded anyway, at 1 oz (35 µm, 0.4926 mΩ/sq at 20 °C):

| candidate | length | width | R | ΔV @1.5 A | ΔV @1.75 A | I²R @1.5 A | I²R @1.75 A |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| short trunk (clean board) | 19.878 mm | 1.50 mm | **6.53 mΩ** | 9.79 mV | 11.42 mV | 14.7 mW | 20.0 mW |
| short trunk at the floor | 19.219 mm | 1.20 mm | 7.89 mΩ | 11.83 mV | 13.81 mV | 17.8 mW | 24.2 mW |
| **long outer (002U, needs the D9 reservation)** | **45.467 mm** | 1.50 mm | **14.93 mΩ** | **22.40 mV** | **26.13 mV** | **33.6 mW** | **45.7 mW** |
| V1's 0.60 mm path | 53.916 mm | 0.60 mm | — | — | — | — | — | **inadmissible: below the 1.20 mm floor** |

Add ~16 % at 60 °C. **The long outer trunk costs 2.29× the resistance of the
short one — 12.6 mΩ more, 12.6 mV more drop at 1.5 A and 18.9 mW more
dissipation** — and it is not currently reachable without a reservation.

## 8. Section 22 — decisive local gate

**FAIL**, on the trunk and on `BAT_RAW`. Passing: six-layer architecture, both
GND planes, the frozen functional placement plus the TP17 move, Q3.3 POFV with

`track_dangling`, zero `via_dangling`, zero new DRC classes.**

## 9. Decision required

The lever list is now empty of low-risk placement options, and the measurements
say why:

1. **The obstacle is the control field on B.Cu, and `LTC_OV` is the biggest
   single contributor.** `LTC_OV` is a **control** net, not `BAT_MAIN`, so D-264
   already permits it on In2/In3 — moving it off B.Cu authorises no
   high-current exception and relaxes no rule. Running with the B.Cu preference
   released was measured and the router still chose B.Cu on its own, so this
   needs an explicit **control-layer directive**, which is a CTO call.
2. **`BAT_MAIN routed clearance` is still scoped by net name.** Scoping it to the
   current path role — the fourth and last property to get the D-249 treatment —
   is what closes `R77.1 → R79.1`.
3. **The long outer trunk remains available only with a D9 exit reservation**,
   and costs 2.29× the resistance. If it is to be the answer, the D-267
   reservation has to be authorised as the first segment of the final route.

**Inner-layer or via-bridged high-current trunking was NOT tested**, per §19.

**U19 NOT SEARCHED. Phase A NOT run. Phase B NOT run. Converter routing NOT
