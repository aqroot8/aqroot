# FBV2-P2-002X — D-270: path-role offload delivered and proven, and the western-margin blocker is current-carrying, not low-current

**Date:** 2026-08-27 · **Task:** FBV2-P2-002X · **Starting HEAD:** `6edc34a`
**Verdict:** **DECISION STOP.** The D-270 path-role offload mechanism is
**delivered and regression-proven** — a bounded low-current TAP branch on a
power-named net may take In2/In3 inside its own corridor, and every
current-carrying role is still barred. But the offload **does not open the
trunk**, and the reason is measurable by individual path role: the binding
blocker of `R75.2 → D9.1` is the **`BAT_SENSE` 1.00 mm shunt current path**, a
current-carrying role D-270 correctly refuses to move. No bounded low-current
offload set of any cardinality re-opens the trunk on any prefix reproducible at
HEAD. The authoritative PCB is unchanged.

---

## 1. D-270(a) — offload is the fifth property to follow path role

> **Western-margin offload candidate selection shall be by individual CURRENT
> PATH ROLE / ROUTED BRANCH, not whole net or net class. A bounded LOW-CURRENT /
> TAP branch on `BAT_RAW` or `BAT_MAIN` may be offered In2/In3 offload despite
> its power net name. This is not a blanket inner-layer exception. Every
> current-carrying role stays on outer 1 oz.**

D-249 ruled **width** by path role, D-264 ruled **layer**, D-267 ruled **via
geometry**, D-269 ruled **clearance**. D-270 is the same shape one property
over, and it is the property D-269's own audit asked for: the two microamp
`BAT_RAW` divider bridges D-269 pushed into the trunk's margin were excluded from
the offload candidate list **by net class** — `BAT_RAW` is a power net — while
**by path role** they are exactly the low-current copper an offload would move.

**The mechanism.** D-264 emits `BAT_MAIN is outer-layer only` for In2 and In3
with two bounded exceptions — the Kelvin sense corridors `BAT_SENSE_KELVIN` and
`BAT_PROT_TAP_U18`. D-270 adds the **authorised offload corridors** to that same
exclusion, and to nothing else, through `path_role_dru.INNER_OFFLOAD_AREAS`,
which the router sets from the D-270 offload set. A control-signal branch carries
no corridor (it is not `BAT_MAIN` and In2/In3 was never barred to it); only a
power-net branch names one. The router hop is `battery_route_plan.D270_SETS` +
the offload block in `route_battery_block.run_once`: the named branch hops
`B → In2/In3 → B` on the **smallest via its own netclass admits** — the D-267
**0.65/0.40** POWER via, never the trunk's 0.80/0.40 — and returns locally.

**Nothing current-carrying moves.** The trunk, the `BAT_SENSE` current path,
`BAT_MID`, `BAT_CONNECTOR_P` are named in the router's blacklist and rejected by
the DRU on In2/In3 as before. D-270 grants inner-layer authority to bounded
low-current corridors and to no other copper.

## 3. Section — the offload study, by INDIVIDUAL BRANCH

`offload_probe_002x.py` is the instrumentation §1 of the brief asked for. The
router (`AQROOT_BRANCH_TRK`) records the B.Cu tracks **each** `(net, a, b)`
connection lays; the probe then **cuts one routed branch's B.Cu and no other**
and re-asks whether the 1.50 mm (then the 1.20 mm floor) trunk finds a corridor.
It never models a candidate as whole-net deletion. Candidates are classified by
path role: the seven control-signal nets **and** the D-270 addition of the
bounded low-current `BAT_RAW` divider branches; the current-carrying trunk,
`BAT_SENSE`, `BAT_MID`, `BAT_CONNECTOR_P` and the `BAT_RAW` node/reservoir are
refused as candidates.

**17 low-current branches sit in the trunk corridor.** The result is decisive
and it is a *failure recorded*, exactly as the brief requires:

| cut | `R75.2 → D9.1` at 1.50 | at 1.20 |
|---|---|---|
| baseline (no cut) | blocked | blocked |
| **all 17 low-current candidates** | **blocked** | **blocked** |
| candidates + all `BAT_RAW` node/caps | blocked | blocked |
| candidates + all `BAT_RAW` + **`BAT_SENSE`** | **1.50 mm, 20.416 mm** | 1.20 mm |

**No offload set of any cardinality drawn from the low-current corridor
candidates opens the trunk at ≥1.20 mm.** The greedy minimal-set reduction has
nothing to reduce, and the exhaustive cardinality-1 sweep confirms it. The one
cut that opens the corridor is the one that removes **`BAT_SENSE`** — the 1.00 mm
shunt **current path** `Q3.6 → R75.1`, which runs a diagonal wall from
(6.75, 62.45) to (2.80, 66.40) directly between `R75.2` (2.80, 62.04) and `D9.1`
(11.35, 72.50). It is a current-carrying role. **D-270 forbids offloading it, and
correctly so** — at 0.5 oz an inner layer needs 2.73 mm for the 1.5 A it carries.

## 4. Section — the real harness confirms the virtual study

`AQROOT_D270=BRIDGES` on the full driver: the two bridges leave B.Cu, but the
trunk `R75.2 → (D9 stage)` is still **GATE_REJECTED**, on the D-269 clearance —
`BAT_MAIN routed clearance 0.3000 mm; actual 0.2500 mm` — against the `BAT_SENSE`
copper. Virtual evidence **selected** the blocker; the real router **proves** it.
The trunk closes only on F.Cu with vias, which §5 of the D-269 brief already
refused by default. This is the D-263 finding stated once more and now localised
by path role: *there is not enough B.Cu around U18 and R75 for the pin field, the
sense current path and the high-current trunk to coexist* — and the two that
collide, the trunk and `BAT_SENSE`, are **both** current-carrying, so neither can
be offloaded to relieve it.

## 5. Section — the reproduction gap that bounds this result

The 002W qualification prefix (`probe_002w_W3.json`, targets `111111111`, U18
8/8) **does not reproduce at HEAD** with the documented recipe. Every
combination tried — `AQROOT_D256=GSQ/GS/GSQFX`, ± `AQROOT_Q3_POFV`,
± `AQROOT_LTCOV_BCU`, ± `AQROOT_U18_ORDER=6,10,7,1,3,2`, with `AQROOT_D266`,
`AQROOT_D267=F1`, `AQROOT_TRUNK_LAST` — lands at **U18 6/8 or 7/8**, with
`U18.7` the invariant casualty. The cause is measured: the D-266 `U18.8` Kelvin
reservation lands its through via at **(3.000, 71.600)**, not the 002T-proven
**(3.750, 71.600)** ("nearest exit, after retry"). The scored (shortest-via)
site is *accepted* here where 002T's was gate-rejected into the better retry, and
at (3.000) it blocks `U18.7`'s escape — `NO LEGAL ESCAPE … blocked by U18.8
(x22)`. This is a D-269-clearance / reservation-scoring interaction, and it also
routes the `BAT_SENSE` current path into the trunk corridor.

**Why it bounds the verdict, honestly.** D-269's §6 measured, on its 8/8 board,
that removing all control **and** `BAT_RAW` opened the trunk to 1.50 mm without
touching `BAT_SENSE`. On every prefix reproducible at HEAD, removing all control
**and** all `BAT_RAW` still leaves the trunk blocked — it takes `BAT_SENSE` too.
The two results are not both true of one board; they are two boards, and the
board §6 measured is the one that will not reproduce. The offload's *premise* —
that clearing the low-current copper is what the trunk needs — was measured on a
board this task cannot rebuild, and on the board it can build the premise is
false: the binding blocker is current-carrying.

## 8. Decision required

The offload mechanism D-269's audit asked for is delivered and proven. What it
reveals is that the western-margin contention is **between two current-carrying
roles**, the trunk and the `BAT_SENSE` shunt current path, and no path-role
offload can resolve a contention neither side is allowed to leave. The choices
are the ones 002W and D-268 already named, now sharpened:

1. **Reproduce the 8/8 prefix** — resolve the D-266 `U18.8` reservation-site
   interaction so the scored site is rejected into its (3.750) retry — then
   re-measure whether `BAT_SENSE` clears the corridor as D-269 §6 recorded.
   This is a harness/reservation question, in scope for a follow-up task.
2. **Or accept that the margin cannot host both the `BAT_SENSE` current path and
   a ≥ 1.20 mm trunk on B.Cu**, and take the F.Cu via-array bridge or the
   reservation-dependent long B.Cu route (2.29× resistance, quantified in the
   D-267 audit) — a protection-architecture call, i.e. an OWNER decision.
