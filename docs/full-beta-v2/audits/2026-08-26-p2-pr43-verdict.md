## 3. The blocking copper is not `BAT_RAW`

This is the part worth keeping. Measured on the finished board, within 1.2 mm of
each stranded pad:

    U18.7   (5.900, 65.750)  net=LTC4368_FAULT_N
        0.500 mm  BAT_PROTECTED_P   B.Cu  w=0.20
        0.500 mm  LTC_SHDN          B.Cu  w=0.25
        1.000 mm  BAT_SENSE         B.Cu  w=0.20

    U18.10  (5.900, 64.250)  net=LTC_GATE
        0.500 mm  BAT_SENSE         B.Cu  w=0.20
        0.986 mm  BAT_SENSE         B.Cu  w=0.20
        1.000 mm  BAT_PROTECTED_P   B.Cu  w=0.20

**No `BAT_RAW` copper appears beside either pad.** The bridges did not take
U18's lanes. What happened is one step removed:

> PR-43 closes `BAT_RAW` → `LTC_SHDN`, previously `NO_PATH`, now finds a route →
> that route lands 0.500 mm from `U18.7` → `LTC4368_FAULT_N` loses `U18.7`.

So the failure is **not** ordering in the simple sense, and no further reordering
will fix it. **The west margin is oversubscribed.** It has to carry the 1.50 mm
`BAT_PROTECTED_P` trunk, `BAT_SENSE`, `BAT_MID`, the two `BAT_RAW` bridges,
`LTC_SHDN`, and U18's north row. Whichever contender is scheduled last is the
one that fails, and both schedules land on 24 of 29.

**Classification: capacity, not geometry, not width, not ordering.**

## 5. Disposition — PR-43 is flagged, not adopted

`LTC_SHDN`. That trade is not mine to make, which is precisely what §5 Case D

## 6. The decision this puts to the CTO

The two orderings are measured and equal at 24 of 29:

| | PR-43 OFF (default) | PR-43 ON |
|---|---|---|
| `BAT_RAW` | 3 islands, chain severed | **11 of 12 pads one island** |
| `LTC_SHDN` | `NO_PATH` | **connected** |
| `U18` pins | **8 of 8** | 6 of 8 |
| `LTC_GATE` | connected | `U18.10` stranded |
| `LTC4368_FAULT_N` | connected | `U18.7` stranded |
| Kelvin mismatch | 2.454 mm | **0.000 mm** |
| ledger | 24 of 29 | 24 of 29 |

Since reordering only moves the casualty, closing the block needs **capacity**,
and the candidates are:

1. **Move `R80`/`R81` out of U18's north lanes.** §2 deferred this on the
   evidence that R80 is not the *root cause* of `BAT_RAW` — which remains true —
   but the relief being sought now is different: margin for `LTC_SHDN` and
   U18's north row. R80's pose has still never been searched (PR-42), and six
   distinct poses are already generated.
2. **Allow one contender a layer change.** `Q3_CS` and `BAT_PROTECTED_P` are
   zero-via by policy; `LTC4368_FAULT_N` and `LTC_GATE` already carry 2 vias
   each, so a third on one of them is the cheapest concession available.
3. **Accept the four test points open** and treat `TP16`/`TP18`/`TP19`/`TP20`
   as DNP or probe-only. This does not fix `U18.7`/`U18.10`.

My recommendation is **(1) with (2) held in reserve**: it needs no rule
concession, the search already exists, and the U19 question in §6 is in the same
margin and would be answered by the same run.

**Not authorised here.** §5 Case D says stop, and this is the stop.
