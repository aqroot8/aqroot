## 1. The finding, in one paragraph

D-260's rigid-cluster hypothesis is **correct about the cause and does not
produce a solution**, and the reason is now a closed piece of arithmetic rather
than a search result. R75 needs rotation 0/180 for Kelvin symmetry. That
orientation needs a **7.75 mm-wide corridor**. The corridor available between
the board-edge clearance and the divider column is **6.80 mm**. It opens only
when the cluster moves **≥ 0.75 mm east**, and at exactly that shift **D9 —
a functional diode in the protected high-current path — overlaps R77 by
0.170 mm**. The alternative orientation, rot 90/270, bottoms out at **5.132 mm**
Kelvin mismatch against a **5.000 mm** limit: over by **0.132 mm**.

Both routes miss by less than two tenths of a millimetre, and every lever this
task is authorised to pull has been pulled.

## 5. §5 — the R75 corridor, as arithmetic

R75 at rot 0/180 has a **3.875 mm courtyard half-width** and pads at ±2.963 mm.

```
west limit   pad west edge >= 0.500 mm board clearance  ->  R75.x >= 4.075
east limit   courtyard must clear the divider column:
             cluster +0.00  column starts 7.300  ->  R75.x <  3.425
             cluster +0.50  column starts 7.800  ->  R75.x <  3.925
             cluster +0.75  column starts 8.050  ->  R75.x <  4.175   <- opens
             cluster +1.00  column starts 8.300  ->  R75.x <  4.425
```

At the only legal cluster shift (+0.50 mm) the window is **empty**: 4.075 ≤ x <
3.925 has no solution. The window first opens at **cluster +0.75 mm**, where it
is **0.100 mm wide** (4.075 → 4.175) — and +0.75 mm is where D9 overlaps R77 by
**0.170 mm**.

**D9 would need to move east by 0.170 mm to admit +0.75 mm, or 0.420 mm to
admit +1.00 mm.**

### A filter defect found and fixed

The first pass of this search tested courtyards against the board *outline* and
nothing else, and proposed R75 at x = 3.900 rot 180 — a pose whose west pad sits
**0.325 mm** from the edge against a **0.500 mm** board-setup clearance. The
screen rejected **every connection from the first one onward** and routed
nothing at all. The pose filter now applies the edge rule to the **pads**. A
filter that admits a pose the board rejects outright costs a whole screen.

## 6. §5 alternative — rot 90/270, measured with the cluster moved

002N measured the rot 90/270 floor at **5.177 mm** with the cluster unmoved.
Re-measured here with the cluster at +0.50 mm, over the full legal region at
0.25 mm:

```
legal rot 90/270 poses: 120
best mismatch:          5.132 mm   (R75 at 5.75, 65.25 rot 270)
                        maxK 8.201 mm, Q3.6 -> R75.1 8.372 mm
```

**5.132 mm against a 5.000 mm limit — over by 0.132 mm.** The +0.50 mm cluster
shift bought 0.045 mm of it. §2 forbids relaxing the mismatch.

That pose was routed anyway, to see what the rest of the gate does with it:
**U18 4/8** — `U18.2`, `U18.3`, `U18.6`, `U18.7` all open (`LTC_SHDN`
NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE, `LTC4368_FAULT_N` NO_VIA_SITE, `LTC_UV` and `LTC_OV` NO_PATH).
Moving R75 east far enough to improve the mismatch puts the shunt under U18's
escape corridor. **So the rot 90/270 family is worse than it looks: it misses
the mismatch by 0.132 mm *and* costs four control pins.**

One useful result did come out of it: **`LTC_OV R77.2 → R78.1` routed 2.901 mm
on B.Cu with zero vias** — the §11 requirement, met, on the first candidate that
got that far.

## 7. §11 — the LTC_OV fallback lock, and why it exists

002N ended with `LTC_OV` reported as one connected component, and the report was
true and useless: `U18.3 → R77.2` had gone **13.087 mm across F.Cu with two
vias**, exactly the long generic excursion D-256 through D-259 have each refused
for a high-impedance comparator input.

`AQROOT_LTCOV_BCU` now denies LTC_OV and LTC_UV the generic layer fallback
during qualification. The screen either proves a local B.Cu route or reports no
route; **it cannot report a false pass.** Every screen in this task ran with it
set.

## 9. A guard defect, caught by the guard

The placement assertion refused to route K90:

```
PLACEMENT IDENTITY MISMATCH -- refusing to route.
  R75  expected (5.75, 65.25, 270.0)   measured (5.75, 65.25, -90.0)
```

KiCad stores 270° as −90°, so the candidate file and the board described **the
same pose** and the guard fired anyway. Angles are now compared modulo 360.
**A guard that fires on a difference that is not a difference trains people to
ignore it**, which is the one thing this guard must never do — it exists because
002K ran nine screens on the wrong placement.

## 10. §15 — the measured map, and the decision

Every §14 item other than Kelvin has a demonstrated path: PR-47/Q3 POFV,
`Q3_CS`, `LTC_GATE`, `Q3_GATE`, `U18` 8/8, VIN, SHDN, FAULT_N, `R80.1`, `D12`,
`BAT_PROTECTED_P`, the `U11.2` flare and — new in this task — `LTC_OV
R77.2 → R78.1` on B.Cu with zero vias. **The blocker is Kelvin, and it is
geometric:**

| route to the spec | shortfall | what would close it |
|---|---|---|
| R75 rot 0/180 (mismatch ~0.10 mm) | corridor **6.80 mm** available vs **7.75 mm** needed | **move D9 east by 0.170 mm** to allow the cluster +0.75 mm |
| R75 rot 90/270 | mismatch **5.132 mm** vs 5.000 | **relax the mismatch by 0.132 mm**, or a shorter shunt part |

Both are decisions this task is explicitly forbidden to take: §2 pins the Kelvin
mismatch at ≤5 mm, and D9 is not among its authorised levers.

**A third option worth putting on the table because it removes the conflict
rather than trading it:** R75's 5.925 mm pad pitch is what pins the rot 90/270
mismatch and what makes the rot 0/180 corridor 7.75 mm wide. A physically
shorter 15 mΩ shunt with the same rating would relieve **both** at once. That is
a component decision, not a placement one, and it is raised, not taken.

## 11. §19 — opportunity and simplification

- **TP17 and C58 are not the obstruction** — measured, at +1.25 mm and beyond,
  against a +0.75 mm requirement. No test point was moved, and none would help.
- **Minimum ECO displacement** was preferred throughout: the only legal cluster
  shift is +0.50 mm, and the R75 search was seeded at 0.10 mm resolution
  precisely because 002N's failure was 0.060 mm of clearance.
- **`LTC_OV R77.2 → R78.1` at 2.901 mm on B.Cu, zero vias** is the first
  acceptable form that link has taken, and it came without moving R77 at all.
- **No via was introduced by experimentation**; the POFV and D-257 escapes
  remain the only fine vias.
- **The rot 90/270 family should not be re-searched.** It is now measured twice,
  at 5.177 mm and 5.132 mm, and the floor is the shunt's own length.
