## 1. The finding

**D-261's D9 lever works, and it works better than the ruling assumed.** A
**0.600 mm** eastward relocation of D9 — placement only, same part, footprint,
orientation, net, polarity and topology — frees a **+1.00 mm** rigid-cluster
shift, which opens an R75 rot-180 window of **x 4.225 … 4.400** where the
Kelvin mismatch reaches **0.000 mm** with both branches at **7.321 mm**. That is
the first time since 002I that the Kelvin specification has been analytically
satisfiable at all.

**And BAT_SENSE — the blocker standing since 002M — is CLOSED.** On the routed
board `Q3.6 → R75.1` connects at **1.00 mm on B.Cu with zero vias**, and
`BAT_SENSE` is one functional island.

**The gate nevertheless fails, and §17 is the applicable clause:** the placement
geometry is legal and the corridor exists, but the routed result fails for two
different reasons, neither of which is the corridor.

## 4. §5–§7 — cluster and the R75 corridor, derived not assumed

§7 gave an expected window of roughly 4.075 < R75.x < 4.175 and told us to
derive the limits from real geometry. Derived:

```
cluster +0.75, D9 +0.60   R75 rot 0/180 legal x = 4.075 .. 4.150
cluster +1.00, D9 +0.60   R75 rot 0/180 legal x = 4.225 .. 4.400
```

The +0.75 estimate is confirmed. The +1.00 window is new and is the one that
matters, because of a constraint the earlier arithmetic missed:

**THE CORRIDOR HAS TO FIT THE TRUNK, NOT JUST THE PAD.** The first chain found
R75 legal at x = 4.125 with D9 moved only 0.200 mm, and the screen rejected
`BAT_PROTECTED_P R75.2 → D9.1` with `copper_edge_clearance 0.5000 mm; actual
0.4125 mm`. The pad cleared the edge; the **1.50 mm trunk that has to leave that
pad** did not. A 1.50 mm track centred on R75.2 needs 0.500 + 0.750 = **1.250 mm**
of room, so R75.2's *centre* must sit ≥ 1.250 mm from the edge — which is
**0.063 mm more than the +0.75 window can give**. Cluster +1.00 opens it, and
that is what the extra 0.400 mm of D9 displacement buys.

The pose filter now carries that constraint.

## 7. §17 — the exact new first blocker

**`BAT_PROTECTED_P R75.2 → D9.1` is rejected at 1.50 mm on
`copper_edge_clearance 0.5000 mm; actual 0.4125 mm`, and is never retried at
D-249's own 1.20 mm floor.**

The cause is a harness gap, recorded here as **PR-49**: `run()` walks the width
ladder and stops the moment `connect_role` returns `ok`, and the DRC gate runs
*after* that. So a width that routes **geometrically** but fails the **gate** is
abandoned rather than falling to the next rung. `PLAN_1_BPP_TRUNK` carries
`[1.50, 1.20]` precisely so the trunk can drop to its D-249 floor, and that rung
is never reached.

At the chosen pose the 1.20 mm rung would need R75.2's centre ≥ 0.500 + 0.600 =
**1.100 mm** from the edge, i.e. R75.x ≥ **4.063** — which the +0.75 window
(4.075 … 4.150) satisfies. **The rung that would close the trunk exists and is
legal; the harness simply does not try it.**

The second blocker is the **routed Kelvin detour**: analytic 7.378 / 7.267
becomes routed **18.764 / 7.886**. This is the same class 002L recorded on the
authoritative pose (4.948 mm of detour) at a much larger scale, and it is a
routing problem, not a placement one — the placement geometry is inside spec by
a wide margin.

Per §17 nothing further was changed: R75's part is untouched, Kelvin is not
relaxed, Q3 did not move, the ECO was not broadened, and the stack architecture
is unchanged.
