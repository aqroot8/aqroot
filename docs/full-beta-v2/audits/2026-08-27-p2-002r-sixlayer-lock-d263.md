## PART B — D-263 routing (DECISION STOP)

Two scratch screens on the frozen 002P/002Q placement, both with the placement
fingerprint asserted. No placement was searched.

### B1. §10's reordering was implemented and it moves the casualty

§10 ruled the trunk must adapt to proven control geometry rather than erase the
pin-field exits. Implemented as `AQROOT_TRUNK_LAST`: U18's pin field, the Q3 row
and the trip network route first; then the Kelvin pair, the BAT_MAIN chain, and
the BPP trunk last.

It works as an ordering — and the casualty simply changes ends:

| order | U18 | who loses |
|---|---|---|
| trunk first (002Q) | 5/8 | `U18.2`, `U18.3`, `U18.7` — control pins |
| Kelvin first (002Q) | 4/8 | four control pins |
| **controls first (002R)** | **5/8** | `U18.6` **and the sense pads**: `R75.1`, `R75.2`, `Q3.5`, `Q3.6` all `NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE` |

With controls routed first, `LTC_UV` closes at 9.889 mm, `LTC_OV` closes as one
component (`R77.2 → R78.1` at **2.457 mm on B.Cu, zero vias**), `FAULT_N` closes
all four functional pads, `LTC_GATE` closes all six, VIN closes at 4.910 mm —
**and `LTC_SHDN U18.6 → R80.2` returns `NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE`**, so §11's
control-lane reservation never reaches 8/8. The control copper then seals the
sense pads, and `BAT_SENSE` ends in five islands.

**§11's precondition is not met, so the reservation cannot be captured.**

### B2. The paired-internal Kelvin is blocked by a standing rule, not by geometry

§13 authorised using the six-layer stack to lift the sense pair off scarce B.Cu,
and §14 asked for both branches on the same internal layer with ordinary
0.35/0.20 through vias. Implemented (`AQROOT_KELVIN_INNER=I2`) and measured:

```
BAT_PROTECTED_P U18.8 -> R75.2   GATE_REJECTED
  items_not_allowed: 2
  "Items not allowed (rule 'BAT_MAIN is outer-layer only')"
```

**`BAT_MAIN is outer-layer only` applies to the whole net, and
`BAT_PROTECTED_P`'s Kelvin tap is part of that net.** The rule was written for
the 1.5 A current path — at 0.5 oz an inner layer needs 2.73 mm for 1.5 A at a
10 K rise, which is the board's own arithmetic — and it makes no exception for a
nanoamp sense branch that happens to share the net name.

So §14's primary topology is unavailable for one of the two branches **under the
standing rules**, and §17 forbids accepting the asymmetric F.Cu route 002Q
produced. A Kelvin pair with one branch internal and one branch outer is not a
matched pair either. **This is a rule-scope question, and D-263 does not
authorise relaxing `BAT_MAIN is outer-layer only`.**

### B3. A via-override defect found and fixed on the way

The first internal-Kelvin attempt was rejected with `via_diameter ... board
setup constraints min 0.5000 mm; actual 0.3500 mm`. The FINE_ESC corridor that
carries the D-257 via-geometry override was allocated only when a row had **no**
area of its own — and the Kelvin rows carry `BAT_SENSE_KELVIN` and
`BAT_PROT_TAP_U18`. The via override now attaches to the corridor the row
already has, rather than demanding a second one.

### B4. §26 gate

Not reached. §11's control reservation fails at `LTC_SHDN`, and §14's Kelvin

## The findings worth carrying forward

**Every routing order on this placement loses something, and the loser is
whoever routes last.** Trunk first costs control pins. Kelvin first costs more
control pins. Controls first costs the sense pads and `LTC_SHDN`. Three orders,
three different casualties, one cause: **there is not enough B.Cu around U18 and
R75 for the pin field, the sense pair and the high-current chain to coexist**,
and reordering only chooses who goes without.

**The six-layer stack cannot relieve it while `BAT_MAIN is outer-layer only`
covers the sense taps.** The one class of copper that could legitimately move
inward — nanoamp Kelvin branches carrying no pack current — is held on the outer
layers by a rule aimed at the 1.5 A path.

---

## What the CTO is being asked

1. **Scope `BAT_MAIN is outer-layer only` to current-carrying copper.** The
   rule's own justification is a 1.5 A/10 K current-density calculation. A
   Kelvin sense branch carries nanoamps. Scoping it — for instance by excluding
   copper inside the D-249 sense corridors, which are already bounded and named
   — would let §14's paired-internal Kelvin be tried at all. **This is a
   protection-architecture rule and is not this task's to change.**
2. **Then re-run §§11–22.** With the sense pair off B.Cu, the control-lane
   reservation has a genuine chance to reach 8/8, and only then does the trunk
   corridor search of §20 mean anything.
3. **`LTC_SHDN U18.6 → R80.2` needs its own look** — it is `NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE`
   even with controls routed first and nothing else on the board yet.
