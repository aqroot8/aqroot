The route is drawn in the board on **`User.4`** as a polyline, and the channel it lives in is
reserved as **`COAX_915_CHANNEL`**, doc X −1.500 … 6.000, Y 24.000 … 110.000.

**Two documented crossings, both acceptable and both recorded:**

* the coax passes **over `MK1`'s can**. `MK1` is a bottom-port MEMS microphone: the acoustic path
  leaves through the Ø1.05 mm NPTH to the FRONT face, and the part's top surface is a sealed metal
  lid. Nothing acoustic is affected. The enclosure should still carry a 1.0 mm rib so the coax
  does not bear on the lid — an assembly note, not a new feature;
* the **433 lead crosses the 915 coax once**, at about doc (4.5, 21.5), on top of `U8`. Two
  shielded 50 Ω coaxes crossing away from any radiating element. **C-6 forbids crossing a
  radiating element, not another cable**, so this is compliant.

| object | how far inside | note |
|---|---|---|

### 5.1 Why the centre moved

The cavity is 75 mm wide. The Ø58 exclusion is 58 mm wide and `J5` owns 12.1 mm on the right, so
**at most 4.9 mm of lane exists, and only if the exclusion is pushed as far right as `J5` allows.**
With the P1-001 centre at (24.5, 126.0) the exclusion reaches x = −4.5 — past the cavity wall — and
there is no coax lane at any y between 110 and 142. The +6.30 mm shift is the whole solution; it is
bounded above by `J5` (5.490 mm to the loop perimeter, rule ≥ 5) and below by the coax.

Both parts are flat-mount leaded devices whose optical axis is normal to the **board**, so both are
**formed 90° at assembly**. The forming requirement is specified in
[`../assembly/IR_LEAD_FORMING.md`](../assembly/IR_LEAD_FORMING.md).

**Writing the forming requirement found a defect the P1-001 placement could not see.** A formed
`TSAL6100` occupies, measured from its pads in +Y: 0.6 mm of bend radius + **2.0 mm of straight
lead, which is Vishay's stated minimum between the epoxy case and the bending point** + the
**8.7 ± 0.3 mm** body = **up to 11.6 mm**. At P1-001's y = 143.600 the dome would have finished at
y ≈ 155.2 — **1.7 mm outside the enclosure's external top face.** `D1` was therefore moved south
to **y = 141.400**, the northernmost position at which the dome lands on y = 153.0. `U6`'s formed
`TSOP38238` needs only ≈ 9.0 mm and **fits where it already was.**

**Consequence, recorded:** at y = 141.400 the `D1` leadframe is **2.854 mm inside the Ø58 metal
exclusion**, i.e. **2.146 mm outside the Ø48 loop perimeter** against a 5 mm target. `D1` cannot
move east — it is already at the exact X the ≥ 15 mm IR TX↔RX rule allows given `U6` is hard
against the right board edge — and it cannot move north without breaking the enclosure. **The
intruding metal is two 0.5 mm leads and a reflector cup of a few mm², perpendicular to the antenna
plane, in the FRONT cavity while the antenna is bonded to the REAR shell**, so the true 3-D
separation is considerably larger than this plan-view figure. Recorded, not hidden.

## 9. ESP32 thermal vias — P1-O5 resolved

**P1-O5's premise was wrong.** The board's global floor is `min_through_hole_diameter = 0.20 mm`,
not 0.30 mm, so the stock `ESP32-S3-WROOM-1` footprint's **twelve 0.20 mm drills in 0.60 mm pads on
pad 41** raise **zero** DRC violations and always did. The twelve errors the FBV2-P1-001 audit
attributed to them were the twelve `copper_edge_clearance` errors on `J5`, now fixed (§3 row 4).

| item | value |
|---|---|
| Manufacturer geometry | **12 × Ø0.20 mm drill in Ø0.60 mm pad**, `pad_prop_heatsink`, net GND — Espressif recommended land pattern, carried by the stock KiCad footprint |
| Annular ring | **0.200 mm** — above the board's 0.125 mm floor |
| **Fabricator capability** (JLCPCB multilayer, verified live 2026-08-24) | minimum via hole **0.15 mm**; surcharge applies only to *"0.2 mm or 0.25 mm hole size with a via diameter **less than 0.45 mm**"* |
| **Verdict** | **SUPPORTED on the intended JLC04161H-7628 4-layer 1.6 mm stack at no premium** — the 0.60 mm pad is above the 0.45 mm surcharge threshold |
| Local rule added | `.kicad_dru` §15, **`FP-U1 ESP32-S3-WROOM-1 manufacturer thermal-pad vias (0.20 mm drill)`**, `hole_size min 0.20 max 0.20`, scoped to `A.memberOfFootprint('U1') && A.Pad_Number == '41'` |
| Global minimum | **NOT lowered.** The rule is a **guard**: if the global floor is ever raised to 0.30 mm for routing vias, the manufacturer's thermal array stays legal instead of being silently "corrected" away |

## 11. Retention — TWO M2 positions, and that is the honest number

| boss | doc (x, y) | where | keep-out |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`BOSS1`** | **40.000, 12.000** | bottom edge, between USB-C and the ESP32 body | Ø4.5 mm, Ø2.2 NPTH |
| **`BOSS2`** | **59.000, 145.000** | top edge, **inside the mandatory full-height IR barrier** | Ø4.5 mm, Ø2.2 NPTH |

**Search result: Ø6.0 mm keep-out — ZERO legal sites. Ø4.5 mm keep-out — TWO.**

The arithmetic is not a preference:

* a boss is a through-board feature and must clear **both** faces;
* the **display** owns X 3.39 … 59.93 for Y 55.04 … 140.00 on the front;
* the **battery** owns X 6.00 … 66.00 for Y 23.50 … 98.50 on the rear;
* together they leave a **3.39 mm** left sliver and a **4.00 mm** right sliver — both narrower
  than a Ø4.5 keep-out — so **only the 23.5 mm bottom band and the 8 mm top band can host a
  through-board screw at all**;
* the bottom band yields exactly one site once microSD, USB-C, `J6`, the ESP32 body and both radio
  modules are honoured;
* the top band yields exactly one, in the 5.0 mm gap between the two IR windows;
* the top-left corner and the left margin are now the **915 coax channel**, which is mandatory.

**Ø4.5 is justified, not inflated:** the moulded M2 boss OD is 4.0 mm, so Ø4.5 is that plus
0.25 mm per side. Ø6.0 would be an arbitrary 50 % oversize with nothing behind it.

**Structural support is completed by the enclosure, and needs no PCB holes:**

| feature | where |
|---|---|
| Moulded **edge-capture rails** | continuous on the **right** and **bottom** board edges; on the **left** edge segmented to clear the 433 flex (Y 1.5 … 48.5) and the coax channel's western excursion (Y ≈ 112 … 137) |
| Rear non-metallic **support ribs**, bearing on reserved component-free pads | `RIB_R1` X 66.20 … 69.70, Y 24.00 … 44.00 · `RIB_R2` X 66.20 … 69.70, Y 45.00 … 64.00 · `RIB_R3` X 66.20 … 69.70, Y 76.00 … 97.00 · `RIB_B1` X 44.00 … 47.60, Y 21.20 … 23.30 |
| Battery | **no compression** — every rib is outside the battery shadow |
| NFC | no metal in the Ø58 exclusion; every rib is non-metallic and all four are far outside it |

All four rib regions are drawn on **`User.3`** and were verified component-free on the rear face
including every through-hole lead. **No copper pad was created for plastic support.**

## 13. Display offset — accepted

**The display module sits 3.34 mm LEFT of the board centreline, and that is INTENTIONAL.**
Recorded, accepted and closed by the CTO (D-224). It was **not** corrected, and specifically:
the display was not raised, the PCB was not widened, `J5` was not moved and the enclosure width
was not changed. The current display/`J1` relationship is unchanged.

**The display Z stack is UNCHANGED.** The FBV2-P1-001 recommendation to raise the display support
by ≈ 3 mm as the primary 915 solution is **rejected and withdrawn** — the circular NFC geometry
closed the feed without spending any of Column A's Z margin.
