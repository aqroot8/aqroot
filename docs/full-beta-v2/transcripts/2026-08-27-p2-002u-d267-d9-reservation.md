## 4. Sections 15–17 — the TAP path role, and what the rules actually allow

002T's finding was that `R79.1 → R80.1` failed `NO_VIA_SITE — no via site of
0.80 mm reachable on B`, because a microamp divider tap was inheriting the
trunk's via. That much is confirmed and fixed: **via geometry is now selected by
PATH ROLE, not by net** — TRUNK 0.80/0.40, SIG 0.60/0.30, TAP/SENSE their own.

**But the ruling as written is narrower than it looks, and the board is the
reason.** Every smaller via was tried and every rejection was correct:

| via tried | rejected by | rule |
|---|---|---|
| 0.35 / 0.20 | `via_diameter` | board minimum 0.50 mm |
| 0.50 / 0.25 | `drill_out_of_range` | "POWER-class vias use the 0.40 mm drill" |
| 0.50 / 0.40 | `annular_width` | "Via annular ring floor", 0.125 mm |
| **0.65 / 0.40** | — | **legal: 0.40 mm drill + two 0.125 mm rings** |

So a TAP on a POWER net **cannot** be given a small via — the netclass forbids
it. What it can be given is the *smallest legal one*, **0.65/0.40**, and **outer
layers only**, which keeps section 16's prohibition on a new `BAT_RAW`
inner-layer exception intact. With that:

- **`R79.1 → R80.1` ROUTES — 4.532 mm at 0.20 mm, F.Cu, 2 vias.**
- `D12.1 → R77.1` improves from **28.058 mm at 0.60 mm** to **13.682 mm at
  0.30 mm on B.Cu with zero vias**.

**Forbidding the hop outright was tried first and measured WORSE than 002T:**
`R77.1` *and* `R79.1` both fell out of the `BAT_RAW` island, where 002T had kept
`R77.1`. Section 16's "zero vias or stop" is the right instinct and the wrong
mechanism — the outer-layer restriction is what carries its intent.

`R77.1 → R79.1` remains rejected on `BAT_MAIN routed clearance` and `BAT_RAW`
stands at two functional groups: `{C59.1, F1.2, Q2.7, Q2.8, R79.1, R80.1, R86.2,
R89.1}` and `{D12.1, R77.1, U18.1}`.

## 6. What this says about the next ruling

D-266's own logic generalises one step further than D-267 took it. `D9.1` was
treated as a scarce **pad**; the measurement above says the trunk is a scarce
**path** — a continuous 19–20 mm current corridor that the control field consumes
in the middle, not at its ends. The consistent extension is to reserve the
**whole trunk corridor** early rather than its exit.

002Q measured that early-trunk order costing three control pins — but that was
before D-266 existed, and the Kelvin exits are now reserved rather than raced
for. It was repeated on the current tree, and **the hypothesis is measured FALSE.**

is a genuine choice between three things nobody has yet authorised: give the
trunk a bounded layer exception where it crosses the pin field, move `TP17`/`C58`
out of the western margin, or accept a longer trunk that leaves the margin
entirely. All three are protection-architecture or placement decisions.
