## PR-47 — why ordinary vias cannot work

Q3 is `SOIC-8_3.9x4.9mm_P1.27mm`: **1.270 mm pitch, 1.950 × 0.600 mm pads,
0.670 mm copper gap.** `Q3_CS` owns pins 1/3, `LTC_GATE` owns 2/4, one B.Cu slot.

```
Q3.3  NO LEGAL ESCAPE at 0.25, 0.20 AND 0.15 mm
      blocked by Q3.2 (x27), Q3.4 (x20) and track
via 0.35/0.20  all widths  NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE
via 0.25/0.15  all widths  NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE
```

**A via needs a landing site; a landing site has to be reached from the pad.**
`Q3.3` cannot emit legal copper in any direction at any legal width, so the
via's size is irrelevant. Placement, footprint and via-in-pad were all left
untouched first, as §12 requires.

**§13, geometry only:** a filled/capped through via-in-pad at **0.35/0.20 fits
inside a 0.600 mm pad with 0.125 mm of pad copper each side**, leaves the
0.670 mm pad gap unchanged, and puts adjacent drills 2.540 mm apart. Feasible —
and premium. **§14 alternative, recorded not taken:** a Q3 toe extension of
**≥ 0.40 mm** would also solve it and needs its own datasheet ruling.

## §15 — six-layer assessment (triggered, non-destructive)

1.6 mm is retainable and **P1's mechanical evidence stays valid**. Two extra
signal layers and a second GND reference relieve U18 directly and help all later
MCU/display/RF routing. **But it does not solve PR-47**: `Q3.3` still has no
B.Cu escape, so it still needs a via inside the pad or a land-pattern change.
Files affected: `.kicad_pcb` stack, `.kicad_pro`, `.kicad_dru` (its In1-only GND
language names one plane), harness layer constants. Revalidation: impedance
rules, both-plane GND guards, DRC/ERC and all five suites.

## The CTO decisions

1. **PR-47** — via-in-pad on 4-layer, or a Q3 toe extension (≥ 0.40 mm), or

2. **U18 Kelvin** — authorise moving **R75**, relax the 5 mm mismatch, or accept.
   The gap is 0.825 mm of placement plus 4.948 mm of detour.
3. **`LTC_OV`** — standing §11 flag: reached F.Cu through the ordinary fallback
   and split `R78.1`. High-impedance comparator input; not the only blocker; not
   moved silently.
