## A. The architecture is locked

**The authoritative PCB is now six layers.** JLCPCB **JLC06161H-7628**, nominal
1.6 mm, 1 oz outer / 0.5 oz inner, In1 and In4 solid GND, In2/In3 internal
signal. No blind, buried or laser vias. Rollback point
`beta-v2-p2-pre-sixlayer-authoritative` pushed at `5f10073` first and verified
on the remote.

Validated after save/reload/refill: 6 layers in order, published dielectrics
0.2104 / 0.4 / 0.2028 / 0.4 / 0.2104, **In1 and In4 each one GND island**, no
pour on In2/In3, **0 signal tracks and 0 signal vias**, outline datum
72.000 × 148.000 mm, **324 of 324 footprint positions identical** to the
pre-lock board, DRC **byte-for-byte the four-layer baseline**, ERC unchanged,
all five suites PASS. Committed and pushed **before** any routing, per §8, so it
survives Part B.

`p1_regression` now guards **both** reference planes and asserts the layer count
— the In1 check was extended, never weakened.

## B. And the routing stops

**§10's reordering was implemented and it moves the casualty rather than
removing it.**

| order | U18 | who loses |
|---|---|---|
| trunk first (002Q) | 5/8 | `U18.2`, `U18.3`, `U18.7` |
| Kelvin first (002Q) | 4/8 | four control pins |
| **controls first (002R)** | **5/8** | `U18.6`, and the sense pads `R75.1`, `R75.2`, `Q3.5`, `Q3.6` all `NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE` |

With controls first, `LTC_OV` closes as **one component on B.Cu with zero
vias** (`R77.2 → R78.1` at 2.457 mm — the first time that has held in this
order), `LTC_GATE` closes all six functional pads, `FAULT_N` all four, `LTC_UV`
at 9.889 mm, VIN at 4.910 mm — **and `LTC_SHDN U18.6 → R80.2` is
`NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE`**, so §11's control-lane reservation never reaches 8/8.

**And §14's paired-internal Kelvin is barred by a standing rule, not by
geometry:**

```
BAT_PROTECTED_P U18.8 -> R75.2   GATE_REJECTED
  "Items not allowed (rule 'BAT_MAIN is outer-layer only')"
```

The rule covers the whole net, and the Kelvin tap is part of that net. It was
written for the 1.5 A path — 0.5 oz inner copper needs 2.73 mm for 1.5 A at a
10 K rise, the board's own arithmetic — and makes no exception for a nanoamp
sense branch that merely shares the net name. §17 forbids falling back to 002Q's
asymmetric F.Cu route, and a pair with one branch internal and one outer is not
a matched pair either.

**No trunk corridor family was generated** — §20 is downstream of a control
reservation that does not exist.

## The finding

Three routing orders, three different casualties, one cause: **there is not
enough B.Cu around U18 and R75 for the pin field, the sense pair and the
high-current chain to coexist.** Reordering only chooses who goes without. And
the six-layer stack cannot relieve it while the one class of copper that could
legitimately move inward — nanoamp Kelvin branches carrying no pack current — is
held on the outer layers by a rule aimed at the 1.5 A path.

## The CTO decisions

1. **Scope `BAT_MAIN is outer-layer only` to current-carrying copper** — for
   instance by excluding copper inside the D-249 sense corridors, which are
   already bounded and named. It is a protection-architecture rule and not this
   task's to change.
2. **Then re-run §§11–22.** With the sense pair off B.Cu the control reservation
   has a real chance at 8/8, and only then does §20's trunk corridor search mean
   anything.
3. **`LTC_SHDN U18.6 → R80.2` needs its own look** — `NO_LEGAL_ESCAPE` with
   controls routed first and nothing else on the board.

**No authoritative signal copper or placement ECO was written. U19 NOT searched.
Phase A/B NOT run. Converter routing NOT started. B-34 OPEN.**
