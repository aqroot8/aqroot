IR:
- IR stays internal to the product.
- Do not recommend removing it or moving it to an accessory.

COMMUNITY EXPANSION:
- Current old interface = 26 pins.
- New target = exactly 20 physical pins.
- Never duplicate two physical pins onto the same internal GPIO merely to preserve apparent GPIO count.
- Every signal pin should provide independent useful electrical capability.
- User would like to preserve approximately 14 GPIO-capable signals if technically sensible.
- Investigate a topology such as:
  * ~10 expander/general XGPIO
  * ~4 native/fast ESP32 GPIO
  * I2C SDA/SCL
  * +3V3
  * switched accessory +3V3
  * 2x GND
  = 20 total
- This is NOT yet an approved pinout.
- Perform a real pin-budget against the Beta-DM MCU pin map and expanders.
- Identify which existing ESP32 pins are actually available.
- Identify all conflicts with display, microSD, radios, microphone/audio, IR, NFC, USB, interrupts, boot straps, etc.
- Preserve SPI/UART/PWM/interrupt capability through pin multiplexing where appropriate.
- Explicitly distinguish native ESP32 GPIO from TCA9535 expander GPIO.
- Explain the speed/timing limitations of expander GPIO.
- Determine whether 14 independent GPIO-capable lines is actually the best use of 20 pins.
- Produce 2 or 3 candidate 20-pin architectures and recommend one.
- DO NOT lock or implement the connector yet.

- Keep external I2C unless the audit reveals a serious reason not to.
- Explain what internal devices currently use I2C.
- Determine whether external SDA/SCL should connect directly, through the existing TCA9517A/buffer concept, or through another protection/buffering approach.
- Evaluate hot-plug/backfeed/fault implications.
- Do not blindly copy the old Full Beta external-I2C implementation.

NFC:
- Audit the frozen Full Beta NFC architecture:
  ST25R3916 + its power requirements + NFC boost + antenna considerations.
- Determine what needs to be reintroduced into Beta-DM.
- Pay particular attention to:
  * VDD_IO vs PA rail
  * boost converter
  * startup/default-off state
  * power budget
  * PCB antenna / external antenna implementation
  * rear enclosure keepout
  * battery/metal interference
- Do not design the antenna yet.

POWER/FAULT SAFETY:
Because we may skip physical Beta-DM bring-up and manufacture Full Beta directly, perform a serious design-fault audit.

Identify potential self-damage cases including:
- 5V into 3.3V domains
- regulator overshoot
- USB/battery backfeed
- NFC boost fault/overvoltage
- speaker amplifier faults
- accessory power backfeed
- GPIO contention
- unpowered-device signal injection
- bad startup sequencing
- thermal overload
- incorrect footprint/pin mapping
- RF TX peak-current brownouts
- external connector abuse

For each major rail/subsystem, state what protection or validation gate Full Beta v2 should require.
