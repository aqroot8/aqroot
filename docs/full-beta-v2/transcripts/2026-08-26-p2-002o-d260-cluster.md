## The answer

**D-260's rigid-cluster hypothesis is right about the cause and does not reach a
solution.** Moving U18 *with* R76..R83 does eliminate 002N's three control-lane
clearance failures — the hypothesis is confirmed. It cannot move far enough.

The Kelvin blocker is now closed arithmetic, not a search result:

```
R75 rot 0/180 needs a 7.75 mm corridor
available between board-edge clearance and the divider column: 6.80 mm

west limit                  R75.x >= 4.075   (pad edge, 0.500 mm clearance)
east limit, cluster +0.50   R75.x <  3.925   -> EMPTY
east limit, cluster +0.75   R75.x <  4.175   -> 0.100 mm window, opens
                            and +0.75 is where D9 overlaps R77 by 0.170 mm
```

**Only cluster +0.50 mm is legal.** Beyond it: **D9** blocks R77 at +0.75 and
+1.00; TP17 and C58 only appear at +1.25. So §13's test-point scan has a clear
answer — **the obstruction is D9, a functional diode in the protected path, not
a test point**, and moving it is not a lever this task holds. **D9 would need to
move 0.170 mm east.**

The other orientation was re-measured with the cluster moved: **rot 90/270
bottoms out at 5.132 mm mismatch against a 5.000 mm limit** (002N measured 5.177
unmoved; the shift bought 0.045 mm). Routed anyway, that pose gives **U18 4/8** —
moving R75 east far enough to improve the mismatch puts the shunt under U18's
escape corridor.

**Both routes miss by less than two tenths of a millimetre.**

## Two defects found, both in the tooling

**A filter that admits a pose the board rejects outright.** The first pose filter
checked courtyards against the outline and nothing else, and proposed R75 at
x = 3.900 rot 180 — west pad **0.325 mm** from the edge against a 0.500 mm
clearance. Every connection was rejected from the first one; nothing routed. The
edge rule now applies to the **pads**.

**A guard that fired on a difference that was not one.** The placement assertion
refused K90 because the candidate said `270.0` and KiCad reports `-90.0` — the
same pose. Angles are now compared modulo 360. This guard exists because 002K ran
nine screens on the wrong placement; a guard that cries wolf is worse than none.

1. **Authorise a 0.170 mm eastward move of D9** — enough for the +0.75 mm cluster
